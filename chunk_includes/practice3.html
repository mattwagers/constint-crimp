How to Judge 'Acceptable'

How do you know whether a sentence is acceptable?
acceptable
a sentence that sounds like natural, acceptable English you might imagine a friend or colleague using.
unacceptable
a sentence that contains a clear error that you don't think any English speaker would make

An example of an unacceptable sentence in English is "I spotted the girl that you met her.". "I spotted that girl that you met." would be the way most English speakers would form that sentence.

Here are some more examples:

  • The critic was gossiping that everyone says Mariah will receive the award. (acceptable) or
  • The critic was gossiping about the pop star who everyone knows which trainer divorced. (unacceptable)

We are not interested in you asking yourself whether an English teacher or writing instructor would think the sentence is an example of good writing. We are interested in how native English speakers are able to use language in day-to-day interactions. Are these sentences that fluent native speakers could use?

You'll find that you generally have a strong, natural intuition about whether a sentence sounds OK or not, and we just want you to report that 'gut feeling' without overthinking it.

In this experiment, there are many different ways in which a sentence may be acceptable or unacceptable. In addition, there are no right or wrong answers. Many speakers have different intuitions about whether a sentence sounds right or wrong, and that's perfectly natural. Just pay close attention throughout the experiment, and use your gut instincts.
