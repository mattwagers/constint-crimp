How to Judge 'Acceptable'

How do you know whether a sentence is acceptable?
acceptable
a sentence that sounds right to you as English, even if it is long, unusual, or about a strange situation.
not acceptable
a sentence where something sounds wrong, even if it is just one word, and even if it is the kind of slip people sometimes make when talking fast.

For example, "The dogs barks at the mailman every morning." is not acceptable: barks doesn't fit with the dogs. You would say "The dogs bark at the mailman every morning." The rest of the sentence is fine, but one word is enough to make it unacceptable.

Another example is "I spotted the girl that you met her.", which is also not acceptable. You would say "I spotted the girl that you met."

On the other hand, "The penguin that the zookeeper adores refuses to eat fish on Tuesdays." is acceptable. It is a strange thing to say, but nothing in it is wrong as English.

About half of the sentences in this experiment contain an error, so you should expect to answer No about as often as Yes. Errors are often small and easy to miss while the words are going by, so pay close attention to every word.

We are not asking whether a sentence is good writing or what an English teacher would say. We want your own sense, as an English speaker, of whether the sentence sounds right. Go with your first reaction, but if a word sounded off, trust that and answer No.
