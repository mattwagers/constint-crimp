How to Respond

At the end of each sentence, you'll be asked to give your judgment of whether or not you found the sentence to be acceptable.

    To respond:
  • acceptable, press the 'f' key
  • not acceptable, use the 'j' key.

We chose 'f' and 'j' so that you can respond quickly by resting both hands on the keyboard, with your left index finger on 'f' and your right index finger on 'j'. You may respond with your mouse if you would like as well.

We would like you to respond as quickly as you can, so go with your gut instinct about the sentence. Don't overthink your response!

To help you go with your first impulse, we're giving you a limit of 3 seconds to respond. Using your hands will allow you to be faster in judging the sentence, so we recommend that.
